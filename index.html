NOVIX sits on top of Windows and keeps all your apps working. It just makes the desktop yours.

One bar

Apps, music, weather, calendar, CPU and Wi-Fi in a single bar that slides away when you don't need it.

◐

Live wallpapers

Real videos and animated scenes that loop without a seam, and switch between day and night by themselves.

✦

Customize everything

Drag tiles anywhere, scroll to resize, drop them in the trash. 13 clock styles and your own colours.

⌕

Search & convert

Find apps, search the web, do maths and convert currencies or units right from one box.

Tom, your assistant

Say “Hey Tom” to open apps, change the wallpaper, control music or ask anything.

Game mode

When a game goes fullscreen, NOVIX steps aside and frees its memory, so every frame goes to the game.

Messages from Discord, WhatsApp and other apps collected in one calm list.
